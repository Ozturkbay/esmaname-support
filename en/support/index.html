About Esmaname

Esmaname is an iPhone app for reading the 99 Names of Allah (Esmaül Hüsna) and for counting post-prayer tesbihat and your own dhikr (zikir).

You don’t need an account, and your zikir data is stored only on your device. The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads; it is ad-free with the one-time Premium.

The app is available in Turkish and English and follows your iPhone’s language. To change it for Esmaname only, open iOS Settings > Esmaname > Language, or tap Dil / Language in the app’s Settings tab. In English, the meanings of the Names and the tesbihat texts stay in Turkish, exactly as Diyanet publishes them, with the note “Meaning shown in Turkish from Diyanet sources.” No official English version from Diyanet exists, and the religious texts are never machine-translated.

Screen and button names are given here in Turkish, as they appear in the Turkish version of the app, with the English label or an explanation where helpful (for example Ayarlar = Settings, Zikirmatik = Counter).

Free content and Premium

Esmaname is free to download. The following are free for everyone, permanently:

  • The 99 Names: each Name’s Arabic script, Turkish name and meaning.
  • Namaz Sonrası Tesbihat (post-prayer tesbihat): a guided flow of 33 tasbih, 33 tahmid and 33 takbir, followed by the closing tahlil.
  • Zikirmatik: a simple dhikr counter.
  • Favorites: add Names to your favorites.
  • Daily goal: a daily zikir goal and today’s progress.
  • Daily reminder: one local notification a day, scheduled on your device.
  • Zikirlerim (My Zikirs): up to 3 personal zikirs, each with its own counter and an optional target.
  • Statistics: today and the last 7 days.
  • Appearance: theme mode, text size and the Klasik (Classic) color palette.

Religious content is never behind an ad or a payment. The free version shows Google AdMob ads.

Premium is optional and adds:

  • No ads: no ads are shown at all.
  • Unlimited Zikirlerim.
  • All color palettes: Gece Mavisi (Night Blue), Gül Kurusu (Dusty Rose) and Toprak (Earth).
  • Streak and full statistics: consecutive days you have reached your goal, the last 30 days, all time, your best day and a 14-day chart.

Without buying Premium, you can also unlock one of these customizations for 24 hours by watching an ad (see What does watching an ad unlock?).

Why do I see ads?

Google AdMob ads are shown in the app so that Esmaname can stay free for everyone. Ads are limited:

  • A full-screen ad may appear only when a zikir session starts (when you open a Zikirlerim counter or tap Başla (Start) in Tesbihat) or after one is finished (when you leave a counter that has reached its target or close the Tesbihat completion screen).
  • No ads are shown during the first 10 minutes after you first install the app.
  • Ads are at least 3 minutes apart, with at most 4 per session and 12 per day.
  • There are no banner ads, and no ads when the app starts.

Because of the religious content, ad content is limited to Google’s “G” (general audiences) rating. If you see an ad you think is inappropriate, please email us with a screenshot.

Will an ad appear while I’m counting?

No, never. No ads are shown while you are counting in Zikirmatik, in your Zikirlerim counters or during Tesbihat. An ad may appear only when a zikir session starts or after it is finished. With Premium there are no ads at all.

How do I remove ads?

The one-time Premium purchase (₺149,99 in Türkiye) removes all ads. It is not a subscription; you pay once. With Premium, the app makes no ad requests and the ad consent form is not shown.

To buy it, see Buying Premium. If you have bought Premium before, restore it and the ads go away too.

What does watching an ad unlock?

When you tap Reklam izle (Watch ad) in the app and watch an ad, you can unlock one of the following customizations for 24 hours:

  • unlimited Zikirlerim (up to 50 zikirs);
  • all color palettes (Gece Mavisi, Gül Kurusu and Toprak);
  • streak and full statistics (the last 30 days, all time, your best day and a 14-day chart).

When the 24 hours end, nothing is deleted. If you are using a Premium palette, the app falls back to Klasik; your choice is kept.

These rewarded ads are shown only when you tap Reklam izle.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

Before ads are shown, Google’s consent form appears where required by law (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and regulated US states), followed by Apple’s tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency). To change your choices later:

  • Tracking permission: on your iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn permission for Esmaname on or off.
  • Ad consent choices (where required): in the app, open Ayarlar > Reklam gizlilik tercihleri (Ad privacy choices).

If you decline, ads are still shown; they are non-personalized or limited ads. Premium removes all ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Buying Premium

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase; in Türkiye it costs ₺149,99, and elsewhere the price shown in the App Store applies. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. With Premium, the app is ad-free.

  1. Open the Ayarlar (Settings) tab and tap the Premium card.
  2. Tap Satın Al (Buy). The button shows the current price.
  3. Confirm the purchase on Apple’s sheet (with Face ID, Touch ID or your password).

Payment is taken by Apple. Premium is tied to the Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I restore Premium?

If you have bought Premium before and have reinstalled the app or are using another device:

  1. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
  2. Go to Ayarlar > Premium > Satın Alımları Geri Yükle (Restore Purchases).

Restoring does not charge you again, and once Premium is unlocked the ads go away too. You can unlock Premium on your other iPhones that use the same Apple Account by restoring there too.

Family Sharing: Family Sharing is not enabled for Premium. Premium can be used only with the Apple Account that bought it.

Purchase awaiting approval

If the Premium screen or Settings shows “Satın alma onay bekliyor.” (Purchase awaiting approval), the purchase has not been completed yet. Common reasons:

  • Ask to Buy: a parent in your Family Sharing group needs to approve the purchase.
  • Payment method: Apple is waiting for your payment method to be verified or updated.

Once the purchase is approved, Premium unlocks. If Premium doesn’t appear after approval, close and reopen the app or tap Satın Alımları Geri Yükle. You are not charged for a purchase that is still awaiting approval.

Refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. We can’t issue refunds ourselves; to request one, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Reminders not arriving

The daily reminder is free for everyone and is turned on in Ayarlar > Günlük hatırlatıcı (Daily reminder). It is scheduled locally on your device and doesn’t need an internet connection. If it doesn’t arrive, check the following in order:

  1. Is the reminder on? The Settings screen should show “Her gün” (Every day) and the time you chose.
  2. Notification permission: on your iPhone, go to Settings > Notifications > Esmaname and turn on Allow Notifications.
  3. Focus and Do Not Disturb: an active Focus may be silencing notifications. You can add Esmaname to its allowed apps.
  4. Scheduled Summary: if Esmaname is in your Scheduled Summary, the notification appears with the summary rather than immediately.
  5. Time: make sure the reminder time is correct. If your time zone or device clock changed, turn the reminder off and on again.
  6. If you deleted the app: after reinstalling, you may need to turn the reminder on again and allow notifications.

Where is my data stored?

Your counts, favorites, Zikirlerim, history and settings are stored only on your device: in a local database belonging to the app and, for some items, in the iOS Keychain. None of this is sent to us or to a server, and the app has no iCloud sync feature. The ad-frequency counter and the expiry time of features you unlocked by watching an ad are also kept only on your device.

The app connects to the internet only for ads and the consent form (Google) and for purchases (App Store); everything else works offline. The data Google collects for ads is described in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting the app deletes its data from your device. Some settings may remain in the device keychain. We can’t recover deleted data for you.

Moving to a new iPhone

Download Esmaname for free from the App Store on your new iPhone. For Premium, sign in with the same Apple Account and go to Ayarlar > Premium > Satın Alımları Geri Yükle.

Your data is not transferred automatically. Esmaname has no account or cloud sync. Your counts, Zikirlerim and history move to a new iPhone only if you set it up from an iOS backup (such as iCloud Backup) or transfer data from your old iPhone during setup, and that backup includes the app’s data.

Sources of religious content

The religious texts in Esmaname come from official publications of the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı):

  • Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, “Allah’ın 99 ismi hakkında bilgi verir misiniz?” (16.02.2023);
  • Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, “Namazlardan sonra tesbîhât nasıl yapılır?” (12.07.2017);
  • Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı, İslam İlmihali.

You can see the list of sources in the app under Ayarlar > Kaynaklar (Sources). Tapping Kaynağı Aç (Open Source) opens the relevant page in your browser.

AI is not used to generate or alter the religious texts.

Esmaname is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye.

Reporting a content issue

If you think there is an error in a Name, its meaning or the tesbihat text, please email feesheet.support@icloud.com and include:

  • the Name or the screen concerned;
  • the text you think is wrong and what you think it should be;
  • the source you are relying on, if any;
  • a screenshot, if possible.

We review reports against the sources above and, where needed, correct the text in a future update.

Arabic text display

Arabic text is shown in an Arabic font bundled with the app, so it doesn’t depend on the fonts on your device. If Arabic text looks cut off, overlapping or incorrect:

  1. Update Esmaname and iOS to the latest versions.
  2. Try reducing your iPhone’s text size by one step in Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size.
  3. If the problem continues, email us with a screenshot.

Privacy

Esmaname has no account, no server of ours and no analytics tool of ours; your zikir data stays on your device and is never sent to us. The free version shows Google AdMob ads; for ads, Google’s software may collect some data, such as IP address, device identifiers and ad interactions. With Premium, the app is ad-free and makes no ad requests. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Contact support

Email feesheet.support@icloud.com. To help us help you faster, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the app version (shown at the bottom of the Ayarlar screen);
  • what happened and what you expected;
  • a screenshot, if it helps.

We usually aim to reply within a few business days.
