About Esmaname

Esmaname is an iPhone app for reading the 99 Names of Allah (Esmaül Hüsna), counting your dhikr and the after-prayer tasbih, and following Diyanet’s calendar of religious days and Ramadan. It has widgets and an Apple Watch app.

You don’t need an account; your records are stored only on your device. The free version never shows an ad on its own; an ad plays only when you choose to watch one to unlock a feature for 24 hours. The one-time Premium removes all ads.

Free content and Premium

Esmaname is free to download. Religious content and worship tools are always free for everyone; none of it is behind an ad or a payment:

  • The 99 Names: each name’s Arabic script, transliteration and meaning; favorites.
  • After-Prayer Tasbih: a guided flow of 33 tesbih, 33 tahmid and 33 tekbir, completed by the tehlil.
  • Dhikr Counter and My Zikirs (up to 3 personal zikirs, each with its own counter and optional target).
  • Daily goal and daily reminder.
  • Religious Days & Kandils and their reminders (every day, at the time you choose).
  • Ramadan mode: the Ramadan dates, the fasting log, the teravih counter, and the fitre and zakat calculators.
  • Statistics: today and the last 7 days.
  • Appearance: theme mode, text size and the Classic color palette.
  • The “Esma of the Day” widget (small size).

Premium is optional and adds:

  • Ad-free: no ads are loaded or shown.
  • Unlimited My Zikirs (up to 50).
  • All color palettes: Midnight Blue, Dusty Rose and Earth.
  • Streak and all statistics: current and longest streak, the last 30 days, your best day, totals and your Ramadan summary.
  • Customised religious day reminders: choose which days to be reminded of, and get a reminder the day before.
  • All widgets (see Widgets).
  • The Apple Watch counter (see Apple Watch).

Without buying Premium, you can also unlock unlimited My Zikirs, the color palettes or the statistics for 24 hours by watching an ad (see What does watching an ad unlock?).

When is an ad shown?

The free version never shows an ad on its own. An ad plays only when you tap Watch an ad to unlock a feature.

  • There are never ads while you count (Dhikr Counter, My Zikirs, Tasbih, teravih counter), and none when you start or finish counting.
  • There are no ads on the Religious Days and Ramadan screens.
  • There are no ads on Apple Watch or in widgets.
  • There are no banner ads, and no ads when the app opens.

Because of the religious content, ad content is limited to Google’s “G” (general audiences) rating. If you see an ad you think is inappropriate, please email us with a screenshot.

What does watching an ad unlock?

When you tap Watch an ad and watch the ad to the end, you can unlock one of the following for 24 hours:

  • Unlimited My Zikirs (up to 50);
  • all color palettes (Midnight Blue, Dusty Rose and Earth);
  • streak and all statistics (the last 30 days, your best day, totals; including the Ramadan summary and past Ramadans).

If the ad is closed before it finishes, nothing is unlocked. If you see “No ad is available right now”, please try again a little later.

When the 24 hours are over, nothing is deleted: you can keep counting and editing the extra zikirs you added; if you were using a Premium palette, the app returns to Classic and keeps your choice; statistics are only hidden.

Customised religious day reminders, widgets and Apple Watch can’t be unlocked with an ad; they unlock only with Premium.

How do I remove ads?

The one-time Premium purchase (₺149,99 in Türkiye) removes all ads. It’s not a subscription; you pay once. With Premium, the app makes no ad requests and the ad consent form is not shown.

To buy it, see Buying Premium. If you bought Premium before, restore it.

Ad and tracking choices

If you don’t have Premium, shortly after the app opens it shows Google’s consent form where required by law (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and regulated US states), then Apple’s tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency). These are never shown while a counter is on screen. To change your choices later:

  • Tracking permission: on your iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn the permission for Esmaname on or off.
  • Ad consent choices (where required): in the app, open Settings > Ad privacy choices.

If you don’t allow tracking, “Watch an ad” still works; the ads shown are non-personalized or limited ads. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

Buying Premium

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase; in Türkiye it costs ₺149,99, and elsewhere the price shown in the App Store applies. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

  1. Open the Settings tab and tap the Premium card.
  2. Tap Buy. The button shows the current price.
  3. Confirm the purchase on Apple’s screen (with Face ID, Touch ID or your password).

Payment is taken by Apple. Premium is linked to the Apple Account you buy it with. Apple Watch and the widgets follow the Premium status on your iPhone; you don’t need to do anything else.

Restoring Premium

If you bought Premium before and have reinstalled the app or are using another iPhone:

  1. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
  2. Go to Settings > Premium > Restore Purchases.

Restoring never charges you again, and ads are removed once Premium is on. You can restore Premium on your other iPhones that use the same Apple Account.

Family Sharing: Family Sharing is not enabled for Premium. Premium can be used only with the Apple Account that bought it.

Purchase awaiting approval

If the Premium screen or Settings says “Purchase awaiting approval.”, the purchase hasn’t been completed yet. Common reasons:

  • Ask to Buy: a parent in Family Sharing needs to approve the purchase.
  • Payment method: Apple is waiting for your payment method to be verified or updated.

Premium turns on by itself once the purchase is approved. If it doesn’t appear after approval, close and reopen the app, or tap Restore Purchases. You aren’t charged for a purchase that is awaiting approval.

Refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. We can’t issue refunds ourselves; to request one, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Choosing the language

Esmaname can be used in English and Turkish. To choose, open Settings > Dil / Language and pick System, Türkçe or English. The change applies right away and is remembered.

  • System follows your iPhone: the first of Turkish or English among your iPhone’s preferred languages is used, otherwise Turkish.
  • The widgets and the Apple Watch app follow the language you choose in the app.
  • Texts shown by iOS itself, such as the tracking prompt, and the widget names in the widget gallery follow your iPhone language.

The Turkish religious texts (Arabic script, transliteration and Turkish meaning) are shown unchanged, as Diyanet published them, in both languages. In the English interface, the meanings of the Names and of the tasbih phrases are shown as English translations of Diyanet’s Turkish meanings, labelled as such. Diyanet’s quotes on the Ramadan screens stay in Turkish in the English interface.

Religious days and reminders

Tap the Home > Religious Days card to see religious days and kandils with the dates Diyanet published and a countdown. The list is inside the app; no internet connection is needed.

  • Kandil nights and Kadir Gecesi begin on the evening of the date shown; the app shows this as “Evening of …”.
  • The app includes dates up to the end of 2028. Later years will come with an app update.

Turning reminders on

  1. Go to Home > Religious Days > Reminders.
  2. Turn on Remind me of religious days and kandils and choose the notification time.
  3. When asked, allow notifications for Esmaname.

In the free version, every religious day and kandil is reminded at the time you choose. For a kandil night, the notification arrives on the day the night begins; bayrams are reminded on their first day. With Premium you can choose which days to be reminded of and get a reminder the day before as well. Without Premium your choices are kept, but every day is reminded.

Reminders are scheduled only on your device; nothing is sent to any server.

Ramadan mode

Tap the Home > Ramazan (Ramadan) card. Everything in Ramadan mode is free, and there are no ads on these screens.

  • Dates: first teravih, first fast, Kadir Gecesi (Night of Power), last fast (arefe) and Ramazan Bayramı (Eid al-Fitr); the days left until Ramadan, or which day of Ramadan it is.
  • Fasting log: tick the days you fasted. A day can be ticked on the day itself or later.
  • Teravih counter: tap once every 2 rekats; each night is saved separately. The counter stops at 20 rekats.
  • Fitre calculator: enter the amount per person and the number of people to see the total. You enter the amount yourself; the amount Diyanet announced for the year is shown for reference only, with its source, and is never filled in for you.
  • Zakat calculator: enter the gram price of 24-carat gold, your assets and the debts due within this zakat year; the app works out the nisab (80.18 g of gold) and one fortieth. The app does not fetch prices from the internet.

The calculators only do arithmetic with the amounts you enter; they give no religious rulings. For rulings about your own situation, consult Diyanet or another official source. Enter every amount in the same currency. The amounts you enter and your Ramadan records are stored only on your device.

The short explanations on these screens are quotes taken word for word from texts Diyanet published, shown in Turkish, each with an Open on Diyanet’s site link. The Ramadan summary (days fasted, teravih nights, total rekats, longest streak) and past Ramadans unlock with the statistics, with Premium or for 24 hours by watching an ad; the number of days fasted in the fasting log is open to everyone.

Adding a widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit or +, and choose to add a widget.
  2. Find Esmaname in the list, pick the widget and size you want, and add it.
  3. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, and choose Esmaname in the widget area.
  • If a widget says “Open Esmaname once.”, open the app once after installing it.
  • Without Premium, the Premium widgets show a calm locked view that says “Unlocks with Premium”; tapping it opens the Premium screen. Widgets can’t be unlocked with an ad, and there are no ads in widgets.
  • Widgets read a summary the app keeps on your device and need no internet connection. If a change doesn’t appear, open the app once.

Apple Watch

Apple Watch. The watch app comes with Esmaname on your iPhone (unlocked with Premium). Tap the big button to count; you feel a light tap on every count and a stronger one at every 33 and at your goal. Pick the Counter, the Tasbih after Prayer or one of My Zikirs from the list; you can also count with the Digital Crown. It counts without your phone nearby; the counts join your daily history when the phone is back and Esmaname opens. Add the “Today’s dhikr” complication to a watch face.

  • The watch app needs watchOS 10 or later. If it doesn’t appear on your watch, find Esmaname in the Watch app on your iPhone and tap Install.
  • If the watch says “Open Esmaname on your iPhone once to sync.”, open the app on your iPhone once.
  • Without Premium, the watch says “Premium on iPhone unlocks Apple Watch.” and shows the Esma of the Day. When you buy or restore Premium on your iPhone, the watch unlocks by itself.
  • Counting with the Digital Crown is off by default; turn it on in the settings after choosing a zikir on the watch. The Crown counts only when turned forward.
  • My Zikirs are reset on the iPhone; on the watch you can reset only the plain counter and a tasbih round.
  • There are no ads on the watch.

Reminders don’t arrive

The daily reminder (Settings > Daily reminder) and the religious day reminders (Home > Religious Days > Reminders) are free for everyone and are scheduled locally on your device; no internet connection is needed. If notifications don’t arrive, check these in order:

  1. Is the reminder on? For the daily reminder, Settings should show “Every day at” and your time; for religious days, “All days” or “Selected days” and the time. If it says “On, but notifications are off”, see the next step.
  2. Notification permission: on your iPhone, go to Settings > Notifications > Esmaname and turn on Allow Notifications.
  3. Focus and Do Not Disturb: an active Focus may be silencing notifications. You can add Esmaname to the allowed apps.
  4. Scheduled Summary: if Esmaname is in your scheduled summary, the notification appears with the summary rather than right away.
  5. Time: make sure the reminder time is right. If the time zone or device clock changed, turn the reminder off and on again.
  6. If you deleted the app: after reinstalling, you may need to turn the reminders on again and allow notifications.

Where is my data stored?

Your counts, favorites, My Zikirs, history, fasting and teravih records, the amounts you enter in the calculators and your settings are stored only on your device: in a local database belonging to the app and, for some items, in the iOS Keychain. None of it is sent to us or to a server, and the app has no iCloud sync.

Widgets read a small summary that the app writes to a shared area on your iPhone. Apple Watch talks directly to your iPhone. This data never leaves your devices.

The app connects to the internet only for ads and the consent form (Google) and for purchases (App Store); everything else works offline. Diyanet’s source pages open in your browser only when you tap a link. The data Google collects for ads is described in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting the app deletes its data from your device. Some settings may remain in the device keychain. We can’t recover deleted data for you.

Moving to a new iPhone

Download Esmaname for free from the App Store on your new iPhone. For Premium, sign in with the same Apple Account and go to Settings > Premium > Restore Purchases.

Your data isn’t moved automatically. Esmaname has no account or cloud sync. Your counts, My Zikirs, fasting and teravih records and history move to a new iPhone only if you restore it from an iOS backup (such as iCloud Backup) or transfer data from your old iPhone during setup, and the backup includes the app’s data.

Sources of the religious content

The Turkish religious texts in Esmaname (Arabic script, transliteration and Turkish meaning) are taken word for word from official publications of Türkiye’s Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı):

  • T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, “Allah’ın 99 ismi hakkında bilgi verir misiniz?” (16.02.2023) — the 99 Names;
  • Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, “Namazlardan sonra tesbîhât nasıl yapılır?” (12.07.2017) — the order of the tasbih;
  • T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı, İslam İlmihali — the Arabic, transliteration and meaning texts of the tasbih;
  • T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı (vakithesaplama.diyanet.gov.tr), “Dini Günler 2026, 2027, 2028” — the dates of religious days and kandils;
  • Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, “Kandillerde oruç tutmak isteyen kişi … (gece ve gün hesabı)”, “Teravih namazı kaç rekattır?”, “Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu 2026 yılı fitre miktarını açıkladı” (13.01.2026), “Kimler fıtır sadakası vermekle yükümlüdür?” and “Zekât nedir?” — the quotes in Ramadan mode. The calculator screen links to further pages of the Board.

The English meanings in the English interface are translations of Diyanet’s Turkish meanings, and the app labels them as such. The Arabic script and transliterations are never changed. The Turkish religious texts are never generated or altered by AI.

You can see the list of sources in the app under Settings > Sources. Tapping Open source opens the page in your browser.

Esmaname is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Türkiye’s Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet).

Reporting a content error

If you think there is an error in a Name, its meaning, the tasbih text, the date of a religious day or an English translation, please email esmaname.destek@icloud.com and include:

  • the name of the Name, religious day or screen;
  • the text you think is wrong and what it should say;
  • the source you’re relying on, if any;
  • a screenshot, if possible.

We check reports against the sources above and, if needed, fix them in the next update.

How Arabic text is displayed

Arabic text is shown with an Arabic font that comes with the app (in the widgets too), so it doesn’t depend on the fonts on your device. If Arabic text looks cut off, overlapping or wrong:

  1. Update Esmaname and iOS to the latest version.
  2. On your iPhone, try making Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size one step smaller.
  3. If the problem continues, email us with a screenshot.

Privacy

Esmaname has no account, no server of ours and no analytics; your records stay on your device and are never sent to us. In the free version, “Watch an ad” uses Google AdMob; for ads, Google’s software may collect some data, such as IP address, device identifiers and ad interactions. With Premium, the app makes no ad requests. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Contacting support

Email esmaname.destek@icloud.com. To help us help you faster, please include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (for Apple Watch questions, your watch model and watchOS version);
  • the app version (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen);
  • what happened and what you expected;
  • a screenshot, if it helps.

We usually try to reply within a few business days.
