Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Esmaname: Esmaül Hüsna & Zikir app for iPhone (“Esmaname” or “the app”) handles information.

Esmaname is developed and published by Fatih Öztürkbay, an individual developer. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer. You can reach us using the details under Contact.

Summary

  • Esmaname is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. The one-time Premium purchase removes all ads.
  • Your counts, favorites, custom zikirs (Zikirlerim), history and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • There is no account, and we run no server or analytics of our own.
  • For ads, Google’s software collects some data, such as IP address, device identifiers and ad interactions. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking.
  • The Premium purchase is processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information or usage data through the app. Esmaname has no account, no server of ours and no analytics tool of ours. What you save in the app, such as your counts, favorites and settings, is kept on your device only so you can continue where you left off the next time you open it, and it is never sent to us.

However, Esmaname shows Google AdMob ads so that it can stay free. For this, the app includes Google’s advertising and consent software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform). This software sends some data directly to Google; see section 5 for details. If you have Premium, the app makes no ad requests.

From Google we receive only aggregated AdMob reports that do not identify individual users. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us when you email for support (see Contact).

3. Account, server and internet connection

Esmaname has no sign-in or account system. We run no server, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tool of our own.

The app connects to the internet for only two purposes: to get ads and the consent form from Google, and to talk to the App Store for purchases. The 99 Names, the post-prayer Tesbihat flow, the counters and every other feature work without an internet connection.

Esmaname does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Location Services or other personal data on your device. The only permissions it may ask for are notifications, when you turn on the daily reminder, and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission (see section 6).

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Esmaname shows Google AdMob ads to users who don’t have Premium. Ads are limited so that they don’t interrupt your zikir:

  • Interstitial ads (full screen) may appear only when a zikir session starts (when you open a Zikirlerim counter or tap Başla (Start) in Tesbihat) or after one is finished (when you leave a counter that has reached its target or close the Tesbihat completion screen).
  • Ads are never shown while you are counting.
  • No ads are shown during the first 10 minutes after you first install the app. Ads are at least 3 minutes apart, with at most 4 per session and 12 per day.
  • There are no banner ads, and no ads when the app starts.
  • Rewarded ads are shown only when you tap Reklam izle (Watch ad), and unlock one customization for 24 hours.
  • Because of the religious content, ad content is limited to Google’s “G” (general audiences) rating.
  • Religious content is never behind an ad or a payment.

When you buy Premium, ads are removed completely: the app makes no ad requests and the ad consent form is not shown.

6. Consent and tracking choices

Before any ads are shown:

  1. Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown where required by law (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and regulated US states).
  2. Then Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt appears. Its explanation reads: “Reklamları senin için daha ilgili hale getirmek için kullanılır. İzin vermesen de reklamlar gösterilir; Premium tüm reklamları kaldırır.” (“Used to make ads more relevant to you. Ads are shown even if you don’t allow it; Premium removes all ads.”)

If you decline, ads are still shown, but they are non-personalized or limited ads and your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

You can change your choices at any time:

  • Tracking permission: on your iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ad consent choices (where required): in the app, open Ayarlar > Reklam gizlilik tercihleri (Ad privacy choices).

7. Premium and purchases

Esmaname is free to download. Premium is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase; in Türkiye it costs ₺149,99, and elsewhere the price shown in the App Store applies. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Premium removes all ads and also unlocks unlimited Zikirlerim, all color palettes, the streak and full statistics.

Your purchase is processed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment card details. The app checks whether Premium is unlocked on your device using Apple’s StoreKit and caches the result on the device. Apple provides developers with sales reports that do not identify individual buyers.

8. Notifications

The daily reminder is free for everyone and optional. When you turn it on, the reminder is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No push notification server is used, and no device token is obtained or stored.

You can turn the reminder off in the app, or revoke notification permission at any time in your iPhone’s Settings > Notifications > Esmaname.

9. External links

Esmaname opens the source pages of the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (Diyanet) in your browser only when you tap Kaynağı Aç (Open Source). Those pages are subject to that website’s own privacy practices.

If you tap an ad, the advertiser’s page or the App Store may open. Those pages are also subject to the relevant party’s own privacy practices.

10. Device backups and new devices

Esmaname has no iCloud sync or backup feature of its own. However, as with most iPhone apps, your device’s own backups (such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) may include the app’s data, depending on your settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us, and we have no access to them.

11. What we don’t receive

We do not receive:

  • your counts, favorites, Zikirlerim, history, goal, reminder time or settings;
  • your name, email address or contact details (unless you email us);
  • your payment information;
  • location, device identifiers or usage data that identify you individually.

The data collected by Google’s advertising software (see section 5) goes to Google, not to us. We see only aggregated AdMob reports.

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous app statistics and crash reports. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Esmaname.

12. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Because of how iOS works, some items may remain in the device keychain and come back if you reinstall the app. We cannot recover this data for you.
  • Data collected by Google is kept for the periods described in Google’s own policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer your request and keep a reasonable record. You can ask us to delete them.

13. Children

Esmaname is not directed at children. Because of the religious content, ad content is limited to the “G” (general audiences) rating. We collect no personal information through the app from anyone, including children; for ads, Google’s software may collect the data described in section 5. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device and in the iOS Keychain, protected by iOS and your device security settings, such as your passcode. Because your zikir data is never sent to us, there is no central copy of it held by us.

15. Your rights

For support emails, the data controller is the app’s individual developer, Fatih Öztürkbay. Your zikir data in the app is kept only on your device and never reaches us. The data collected by Google’s software for ads (see section 5) is processed under Google’s privacy policy. Information you share when you email us (your email address and the content of your message) is used only to answer your request.

  • Deleting app data: you can reset counters and remove favorites and Zikirlerim entries in the app; to remove all app data, delete the app from your device.
  • Ad and tracking choices: change tracking permission in your iPhone settings and, where required, your ad consent choices in the app under Reklam gizlilik tercihleri (see section 6). With Premium, ad requests stop completely.
  • Data processed by Google: for your rights over this data and the tools Google provides, see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Backups: manage iCloud Backup and other device backups in your iPhone settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live (for example, under Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK), you may have rights such as asking whether your personal data is processed or asking for its deletion. For any question or request, email us.

This section is for information only and is not legal advice.
