Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Esmaname: Esmaül Hüsna & Zikir app for iPhone and Apple Watch (“Esmaname” or “the app”) and its widgets handle information.

Esmaname is developed and published by Fatih Öztürkbay, an individual developer. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer. You can reach us using the details under Contact.

Summary

  • Your counts, favorites, My Zikirs, history, fasting and teravih records, calculator amounts and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • There is no account, and we run no server or analytics of our own.
  • The widgets and the Apple Watch app work only between your own devices; this data never leaves them.
  • The free version never shows an ad on its own; an ad is shown through Google AdMob only when you choose Watch an ad. For ads, Google’s software collects some data, such as IP address, device identifiers and ad interactions. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking.
  • The one-time Premium removes all ads. The purchase is processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information or usage data through the app. Esmaname has no account, no server of ours and no analytics tool of ours. What you save in the app is kept on your device only so you can continue where you left off the next time you open it, and it is never sent to us.

So that you can unlock a feature for 24 hours by watching an ad, the free version includes Google’s advertising and consent software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform). This software sends some data directly to Google; see section 5 for details. If you have Premium, the app makes no ad requests.

From Google we receive only aggregated AdMob reports that do not identify individual users. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us when you email for support (see Contact).

3. Account, server and internet connection

Esmaname has no sign-in or account system. We run no server, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tool of our own.

The app connects to the internet for only two purposes: to get ads and the consent form from Google, and to talk to the App Store for purchases. The 99 Names, the After-Prayer Tasbih, the counters, religious days, Ramadan mode, the calculators, the widgets, the Apple Watch app and every other feature work without an internet connection. The app does not fetch prices, dates or content from the internet; the religious day dates are built into the app.

Esmaname does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Location Services, Health data or other personal data on your device. The only permissions it may ask for are notifications, when you turn on the daily reminder or the religious day reminders, and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission (see section 6).

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version never shows an ad on its own. An ad plays only when you tap Watch an ad to unlock a feature (unlimited My Zikirs, the color palettes or the statistics) for 24 hours.

  • So that an ad is ready when you tap Watch an ad, the app may load a rewarded ad in the background after the consent steps. Loading sends an ad request to Google; the ad itself is shown only when you choose it.
  • There are no interstitial (full-screen) ads, no banner ads and no app-open ads.
  • There are never ads while you count, on the Religious Days and Ramadan screens, on Apple Watch or in widgets.
  • Because of the religious content, ad content is limited to Google’s “G” (general audiences) rating.
  • Religious content and worship tools are never behind an ad or a payment.

When you buy Premium, ads are removed completely: the app makes no ad requests and the ad consent form is not shown.

6. Consent and tracking choices

If you don’t have Premium, shortly after the app opens, and never while a counter is on screen:

  1. Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown where required by law (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and regulated US states).
  2. Then Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt appears. Its explanation follows your iPhone language and reads: “Used to make ads more relevant to you. Ads are shown either way; Premium removes all ads.” (“Reklamları senin için daha ilgili hale getirmek için kullanılır. İzin vermesen de reklamlar gösterilir; Premium tüm reklamları kaldırır.”)

If you don’t allow tracking, “Watch an ad” still works, but the ads shown are non-personalized or limited ads and your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

You can change your choices at any time:

  • Tracking permission: on your iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ad consent choices (where required): in the app, open Settings > Ad privacy choices.

7. Premium and purchases

Esmaname is free to download. Premium is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase; in Türkiye it costs ₺149,99, and elsewhere the price shown in the App Store applies. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Premium removes all ads and also unlocks unlimited My Zikirs, all color palettes, the streak and all statistics, customised religious day reminders, all widgets and the Apple Watch counter.

Your purchase is processed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment card details. The app checks whether Premium is unlocked on your device using Apple’s StoreKit and caches the result on the device. The widgets and Apple Watch learn this result only from the app on your iPhone. Apple provides developers with sales reports that do not identify individual buyers.

8. Notifications

The daily reminder and the religious day reminders are free for everyone and optional. When you turn them on, the reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS; religious day reminders are worked out from the dates built into the app. No push notification server is used, no device token is obtained or stored, and nothing is sent to any server.

You can turn the reminders off in the app, or revoke notification permission at any time in your iPhone’s Settings > Notifications > Esmaname.

9. Widgets

The widgets read only a summary that the app keeps on your iPhone in the area shared by the app and its widgets (an App Group): today’s dhikr count and goal, your language choice, your Premium status, upcoming religious day dates, the fast days you ticked during Ramadan and the 99 Names. This summary never leaves your device; the widgets don’t connect to the internet and show no ads.

10. Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app talks directly to your iPhone through Apple’s WatchConnectivity: the language, your Premium status, today’s count and goal, My Zikirs and the counts only travel between your phone and your watch. No data is sent to us or to any server. There are no ads on the watch.

The watch keeps the counts you make while your phone is away, and its own settings, on the watch until they sync. The Esma of the Day and tasbih texts shown on the watch also come from the app on your iPhone. The watch app asks for no additional permissions.

11. External links

Esmaname opens pages of Türkiye’s Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet), the source of its religious texts, in your browser only when you tap a link such as Open source or Open on Diyanet’s site. Those pages are subject to that website’s own privacy practices.

If you tap an ad, the advertiser’s page or the App Store may open. Those pages are also subject to the relevant party’s own privacy practices.

12. Device backups and new devices

Esmaname has no iCloud sync or backup feature of its own. However, as with most iPhone apps, your device’s own backups (such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) may include the app’s data, depending on your settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us, and we have no access to them.

13. What we don’t receive

We do not receive:

  • your counts, favorites, My Zikirs, history, goal, reminder times or settings;
  • your fasting and teravih records or the amounts you enter in the calculators;
  • the summary the widgets read, or the data exchanged between your Apple Watch and your iPhone;
  • your name, email address or contact details (unless you email us);
  • your payment information;
  • location, device identifiers or usage data that identify you individually.

The data collected by Google’s advertising software (see section 5) goes to Google, not to us. We see only aggregated AdMob reports.

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous app statistics and crash reports. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Esmaname.

14. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; the watch app’s data is deleted when it is removed from the watch. Because of how iOS works, some items may remain in the device keychain and come back if you reinstall the app. We cannot recover this data for you.
  • Data collected by Google is kept for the periods described in Google’s own policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer your request and keep a reasonable record. You can ask us to delete them.

15. Children

Esmaname is not directed at children. Because of the religious content, ad content is limited to the “G” (general audiences) rating. We collect no personal information through the app from anyone, including children; for ads, Google’s software may collect the data described in section 5. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device and in the iOS Keychain, and the watch’s data in the watch app’s private storage, protected by iOS, watchOS and your device security settings, such as your passcode. Because your records are never sent to us, there is no central copy of them held by us.

17. Your rights

For support emails, the data controller is the app’s individual developer, Fatih Öztürkbay. Your records in the app are kept only on your device and never reach us. The data collected by Google’s software for ads (see section 5) is processed under Google’s privacy policy. Information you share when you email us (your email address and the content of your message) is used only to answer your request.

  • Deleting app data: you can reset counters and remove favorites and My Zikirs entries in the app; to remove all app data, delete the app from your device (and your watch).
  • Ad and tracking choices: change tracking permission in your iPhone settings and, where required, your ad consent choices in the app under Ad privacy choices (see section 6). With Premium, ad requests stop completely.
  • Data processed by Google: for your rights over this data and the tools Google provides, see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Backups: manage iCloud Backup and other device backups in your iPhone settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live (for example, under Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK), you may have rights such as asking whether your personal data is processed or asking for its deletion. For any question or request, email us.

This section is for information only and is not legal advice.

18. This website

This support website’s own code sets no cookies and contains no analytics, tracking code, marketing pixels, external fonts or external scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for how GitHub handles data.

19. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this policy before releasing that version. The current version is always published on this page with a new “last updated” date.
