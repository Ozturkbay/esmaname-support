Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Esmaname: Esmaül Hüsna & Zikir app for iPhone (“Esmaname” or “the app”) handles information.

Esmaname is developed and published by Fatih Öztürkbay, an individual developer. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer. You can reach us using the details under Contact.

Summary

  • Esmaname collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
  • Your counts, favorites, custom zikirs (Zikirlerim), history and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server that receives your data.
  • The Premium purchase is processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details.
  • The app’s App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

1. Information we collect

None. Esmaname does not collect, transmit or share personal information or usage data. The app works entirely on your device; what it saves is kept on your device only so you can continue where you left off the next time you open it.

The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us when you email for support (see Contact).

2. Data stored on your device

Esmaname saves the following on your device, in a local database belonging to the app (SQLite) and, for some settings, in the iOS Keychain. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

Data Esmaname stores on your device
CategoryWhat it contains
CountersCounts in Zikirmatik and in your Zikirlerim counters.
FavoritesThe Names you have added to your favorites.
Zikirlerim (Premium)Zikirs you have named yourself, with their counters and optional targets.
History and statisticsDaily zikir totals; daily goal and streak (consecutive days) information.
SettingsYour appearance and color palette choice, daily goal, and whether the reminder is on and at what time.
Premium statusWhether Premium is unlocked, kept on the device.

3. No account, server or analytics

Esmaname has no sign-in or account system. The app has no server that it sends your data to; no information is transferred to a server run by us or anyone else.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software and does not use your device’s advertising identifier. It never asks for permission to track you.

Esmaname does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other personal data on your device. The only permission it asks for is notifications, and only when you turn on the daily reminder.

4. Premium and purchases

Esmaname is free to download. Premium is a one-time in-app purchase at the price shown in the App Store. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

Your purchase is processed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment card details. The app checks whether Premium is unlocked on your device using Apple’s StoreKit. Apple provides developers with sales reports that do not identify individual buyers.

5. Notifications

The daily reminder (Premium) is optional. When you turn it on, the reminder is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No push notification server is used, and no device token is obtained or stored.

You can turn the reminder off in the app, or revoke notification permission at any time in your iPhone’s Settings > Notifications > Esmaname.

6. External links

Esmaname opens the source pages of the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (Diyanet) in your browser only when you tap Kaynağı Aç (Open Source). Those pages are subject to that website’s own privacy practices.

7. Device backups and new devices

Esmaname has no iCloud sync or backup feature of its own. However, as with most iPhone apps, your device’s own backups (such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) may include the app’s data, depending on your settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us, and we have no access to them.

8. What we don’t receive

We do not receive:

  • your counts, favorites, Zikirlerim, history or settings;
  • your name, email address or contact details (unless you email us);
  • your payment information;
  • your location, device identifiers or usage data.

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous app statistics and crash reports. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by Esmaname.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Because of how iOS works, some settings may remain in the device keychain and come back if you reinstall the app. We cannot recover this data for you.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer your request and keep a reasonable record. You can ask us to delete them.

10. Children

Esmaname is not directed specifically at children. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device and in the iOS Keychain, protected by iOS and your device security settings, such as your passcode. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no central copy of your data held by us.

12. Your rights

For support emails, the data controller is the app’s individual developer, Fatih Öztürkbay. Because Esmaname does not collect or process personal data in the app, data protection laws (such as Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, KVKK) have very limited application to your use of the app. Information you share when you email us (your email address and the content of your message) is used only to answer your request.

  • Deleting app data: you can reset counters and remove favorites and Zikirlerim entries in the app; to remove all app data, delete the app from your device.
  • Backups: manage iCloud Backup and other device backups in your iPhone settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have rights such as asking whether your personal data is processed or asking for its deletion. For any question or request, email us.

This section is for information only and is not legal advice.

13. This website

This support website’s own code sets no cookies and contains no analytics, tracking code, marketing pixels, external fonts or external scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for how GitHub handles data.

14. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this policy before releasing that version. The current version is always published on this page with a new “last updated” date.
