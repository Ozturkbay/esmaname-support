Esmaname

The 99 Names, dhikr and tasbih

A calm iPhone app for reading the 99 Names of Allah (Esmaül Hüsna) with their Arabic script, transliteration and meaning, counting your dhikr and the after-prayer tasbih, and following the religious days and Ramadan. It counts on Apple Watch too.

Türkçe için ana sayfaya gidin.

What’s in the app

The 99 Names
The list of 99 names published by Türkiye’s Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet): each name’s Arabic script, transliteration and meaning. Search the names and add them to your favorites.
Dhikr Counter and My Zikirs
A counter that counts every tap and remembers where you left off. Set a personal target if you like, and name your own zikirs to count each one separately. Every count is added to your daily goal.
After-Prayer Tasbih
A guided flow of 33 tesbih, 33 tahmid and 33 tekbir, completed by the tehlil. The texts are shown as Diyanet published them.
Religious Days & Kandils
Diyanet’s calendar of religious days for 2026–2028, with countdowns and without an internet connection. If you like, get a reminder at the time you choose on religious days and kandils.
Ramadan mode
The Ramadan dates (first teravih, first fast, Kadir Gecesi, arefe and the bayram), a fasting log, a teravih counter, and fitre and zakat calculators. The calculators only do arithmetic with the amounts you enter and give no religious rulings; the relevant Diyanet guidance is quoted, in Turkish, with its source.
Widgets
“Esma of the Day” on your Home Screen; the small size is free for everyone. With Premium, “Today’s Dhikr” on your Home Screen and the next religious day and the day of Ramadan on your Lock Screen.
Apple Watch
With Premium, the counter, the Tasbih after Prayer and My Zikirs on your wrist; it counts without your phone nearby. A “Today’s dhikr” complication for your watch face.
English and Turkish
Choose the app’s language in Settings > Dil / Language: System, Türkçe or English.

The free version, ads and Premium

  • Religious content and worship tools are always free for everyone: the 99 Names, the tasbih, the counter, favorites, the daily goal and reminder, religious days and their reminders, the Ramadan calendar, the fasting log, the teravih counter and the fitre and zakat calculators.
  • The free version never shows an ad on its own. An ad plays only when you choose Watch an ad to unlock a feature for 24 hours: unlimited My Zikirs, the color palettes or the statistics.
  • There are never ads while you count, on the Religious Days and Ramadan screens, on Apple Watch or in widgets.
  • The optional Premium (₺149,99 in Türkiye) is a one-time purchase: it removes all ads and unlocks the Premium features. There is no subscription.
  • There is no account and no server. Your counts, records and settings are stored only on your device.

Sources

The Turkish religious texts (Arabic script, transliteration and Turkish meaning) are taken from official publications of Türkiye’s Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı) and shown unchanged:

  • The 99 Names: T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, “Allah’ın 99 ismi hakkında bilgi verir misiniz?” (16.02.2023).
  • After-prayer tasbih: Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, “Namazlardan sonra tesbîhât nasıl yapılır?” (12.07.2017); Arabic, transliteration and meaning texts: T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı, İslam İlmihali.
  • Religious days: T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı (vakithesaplama.diyanet.gov.tr), “Dini Günler 2026, 2027, 2028”.
  • Ramadan mode: the short quotes on the teravih, fitre and zakat screens are taken word for word from the relevant pages of Diyanet’s High Board of Religious Affairs (Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu), each with a link to its source. They appear in Turkish, exactly as Diyanet published them.

The English meanings shown in the English interface are translations of Diyanet’s Turkish meanings, and the app labels them as such. The Arabic script and transliterations are never changed. The Turkish religious texts are never generated or altered by AI.

The full list of sources is in the app under Settings > Sources.
