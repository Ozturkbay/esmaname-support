Esmaname

Esmaül Hüsna & Zikir

An iPhone app for reading the 99 Names of Allah (Esmaül Hüsna) with their Arabic script, Turkish names and meanings, and for counting post-prayer tesbihat and your own dhikr.

Allah’ın 99 ismini Arapça yazılışları, Türkçe okunuşları ve anlamlarıyla okuyabileceğiniz; namaz sonrası tesbihatı ve zikirlerinizi sayabileceğiniz bir iPhone uygulaması.

  • Privacy Policy Your zikir data stays on your device. Ads (Google AdMob), going ad-free with Premium, and consent choices.
  • Support Help with ads, Premium, restoring purchases, reminders, your data and sources.
  • Gizlilik Politikası Zikir verileriniz cihazınızda kalır. Reklamlar (Google AdMob), Premium ile reklamsız kullanım ve izin tercihleri.
  • Destek Reklamlar, Premium, satın alımları geri yükleme, hatırlatıcılar, verileriniz ve kaynaklar hakkında yardım.

At a glance

  • Esmaname is free to download. The 99 Names, the post-prayer Tesbihat flow, Zikirmatik, Esma favorites and the daily reminder are free for everyone. Religious content is never behind an ad or a payment.
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads. An ad never appears while you are counting.
  • The optional Premium (₺149,99) is a one-time in-app purchase and removes all ads. There is no subscription.
  • No account. Your counts, favorites and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Religious texts come from official publications of the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (T.C. Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı).

The app is available in Turkish and English and follows your iPhone’s language (iOS Settings > Esmaname > Language). In English, the meanings of the Names and the tesbihat texts stay in Turkish exactly as Diyanet publishes them; they are never machine-translated. Screen and button names on this site are given in Turkish, as they appear in the Turkish version of the app.
